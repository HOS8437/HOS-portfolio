01 // WHO I AM

About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student who fell in love with the moment a program finally runs. What started as curiosity about how things work under the hood turned into late nights with compilers, broken code, and the stubborn joy of fixing it.

These days my energy goes into three things: sharpening my C++, learning how the web is really built, and understanding the fundamentals — data structures, logic, and how to think like an engineer.

I believe in learning by building. Every project here, small or big, is a step on that path.
